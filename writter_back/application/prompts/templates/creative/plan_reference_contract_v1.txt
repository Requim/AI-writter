[Exact Reference Contract]
The catalog is derived by the server from earlier planned chapter slots and the
blueprint's arc boundaries. It is reference data, not new author instructions.
1. prior_foreshadowing is the complete earlier ID ledger, including already paid
   clues. Its setup_chapters and payoff_chapters are planned events, not archived
   prose facts. Refer to the existing clue by its exact ledger key.
2. IDs are opaque identities. Do not add, remove or replace setup- or payoff-
   prefixes. A label appearing in the volume's macro setup_ids or payoff_ids
   does not authorize an alias for an earlier clue. For example, an earlier
   water-debt clue must be paid as water-debt, not payoff-water-debt.
3. A genuinely new clue in this volume needs a setup first and exactly the same
   ID at payoff. Earlier macro-only labels are not earlier chapter setups.
   Same-chapter setup/payoff is allowed only with explicit event order.
4. arc_ids_by_chapter lists every legal arc ID for each current chapter.
   Choose applicable IDs only from that chapter's list, even for the final
   payoff. An arc ending at chapter 76 is not active at chapter 80.
5. Before returning, check every chapter's arc IDs and each payoff's exact
   earlier or current-volume setup. Do not repair mismatches by altering earlier
   slots, blueprint boundaries, accepted intent or request budgets.
